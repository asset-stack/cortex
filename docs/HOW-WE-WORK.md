# How we work in Cortex

A short note for the team: who owns a task, what comes first, and where work goes.

## Every task has one home and one owner
- A task lives in one place: the board where the work belongs (for example **Clients › Previews**, linked to the client,
  or **Operations › Team Tasks**). Your Dashboard, Team › your name and the client's page all show that same task, in the
  same table and with the same columns as its board: change it in any of them and it changes everywhere.
- The **Owner** says who is answerable for it. One person, never a group.
- Someone else working on it too? Keep the owner and add them under **Also on**: it shows on their Dashboard (marked
  with whose it is), but it's still the owner's to answer for.
- A quick question for someone? Write `@Name` in an update on the task. They see it in their Dashboard → **Mentions**.
- **Department** says what a task is for (Operations, Marketing, Outreach, Clients, Pipeline). It's the board's own
  unless you change it: on a mixed board like Team Tasks, set it on each task, and filter the board by it.

## Handing work over
- Give someone a task by setting them as its **Owner**, or with **New task** (Dashboard, Team › their name, or a client's
  or deal's page → **Add next step**). Work for the whole team goes to **AS Team**: assign it there and anyone can pick it
  up (set yourself as Owner when you do).
- It sits at the top of their Dashboard under **Waiting on you · from you** until they click **Accept** (starting it
  counts too). Until then you and the team see it as waiting.
- Not accepted after a day? It shows in admins' **Needs attention**.

## Priority says when
| Priority | Means |
|---|---|
| **Urgent** | Today. Drop other work. One Urgent task at a time. |
| **High** | This week |
| **Medium** | This month |
| **Low** | When free |

Anyone can set the owner and the priority. If two things feel Urgent, ask an admin which one is.

## Start the day on your Dashboard
1. **Waiting on you**: accept what's been handed to you.
2. **Today**: your to-dos for the day. **Do today** on a task puts it here.
3. **My tasks**, under Today: everything allocated to you (you're the Owner or Also on), as each board shows it: a
   table per board with the same columns (Task, Updates, Owner, Also on, Status, Priority, Due, Department). Change a
   status, priority or due date right there, click a task to open it, and **Today** puts it on your day. The order, the
   groups and adding tasks are on the board itself (open it from the table's title). Team › your name shows the same
   tables, and everyone can look at anyone's there. Repeating daily work belongs in your routines (it shows on Today),
   not as a task kept In progress.
4. Keep at most **2 tasks In progress**. Finish or pause one before starting another.
5. Stuck? Set the status to **Stuck** and say why in an update. On a team board, an admin sees it in Needs attention.
6. **My notes**, at the bottom, is your own page. It never shows on the Team page, but admins can open staff notes (and
   the SuperAdmin everyone's).

## After a meeting with a prospect or client
Open the deal (**Pipeline**, click its name) or the client (**Clients**) and, on its profile:
1. **Meeting**: the date, who attended, the link to your meeting notes (the Google Doc) and a short summary of what was
   agreed. Anyone on the team who opens the profile can open the notes from there (share the Doc with the team).
2. **Add next step** for each follow-up: a task with an owner, a priority and a due date, kept on **Operations › Team
   Tasks** (linked to the deal). It waits on its owner's Dashboard like any other task, and shows in Team › their name
   and for anyone you put on **Also on**.
3. **Add link** for anything else worth keeping: a demo, deck, proposal or recording.

## Moving a deal along
Pipeline's columns are **Opportunities → Contacted → First meetings → Demos → Proposal sent**. Move a deal when it
happens (drag it, or use its stage list): Proposal sent means the proposal is with them. **Make client** is on Proposal
sent, once they sign. Set each deal's **Lead** when you add it: **LYCO** (co-pitched) or **AssetStack** (direct). Its icon
and coloured edge show on the card, and the All · LYCO · AssetStack filter on Pipeline and Clients shows one side's work.

When a deal is made a client, its profile comes with it. Adding a meeting, note or link needs **Edit** on Pipeline (a
deal) or Clients (a client). A deal's next steps are tasks on Operations › Team Tasks (older ones on Outreach › Sales
Tasks still show), so adding them needs Edit on Operations; a client's are on the Clients boards. Ask an admin if you
only have View.

## Where work goes
- **Operations**: running the business: BAU, finance, development and internal projects.
- **Marketing**: posts, campaigns and events.
- **Outreach**: sales tasks.
- **Clients → Demos / Previews**: demos and design updates for a client. Add it from the client's page so the client is
  set and it shows there.
- **Team**: who has what. Everyone's open work, waiting, urgent and overdue, one card per person.
